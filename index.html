What is Breathwork?

Breathing is such a fundamental part of our lives. Most of the time it is a function that is occurring in the background keeping us alive and we do not need to be continually instructing the lungs to breathe in and breathe out just as the heart continues beating without our instruction. Unlike many other autonomic systems in the body, the breath can be modulated by bringing our awareness to it.

When we are feeling anxious or scared taking a deep breath and letting out a long sigh can help shift our state. Many of us develop breathing patterns that are shallow and unconscious leaving us out of breath or unable to calm down when our nervous systems gets particularly activated.

Through in person or virtual one on one sessions I work with specific breathing patterns that help develop and bring awareness to the quality of your breath and a direct experience of how changes to our breath brings greater vitality, insight and a fuller expression of our lived experience. In a one on one session I work with clients to identify areas of growth, resourcing or healing that they are bringing to the practice. I guide clients through an active breath practice supporting them through the sensations and experiences that arise.

Typically in an hour session you will breathe for between 20 to 45 min which includes both active breathing followed by rest and integration. The length and shape of these sessions are adapted and customized to each client's needs; it is important to arrive where you are rather than pushing beyond your current capacity. Change, growth and healing comes through patience, compassion and also the diligent work of showing up for ourselves.
